# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Andrew Lee's personal creation and knowledge website: drawing, UI, technology and programming. Personal exploration takes priority over a resume-first portfolio.

## Operating Context
Author content in Markdown and image files using templates. English interface; each entry may be Chinese or English, without mandatory translations. Existing React/Vite site remains the implementation baseline.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Home: short introduction, content entrances in the first viewport, featured drawings and latest writing. Gallery: Drawing only, individual multi-image pages with descriptions, alt text and optional captions; no public completion/status labels. Writing: Article or Note, overlapping Drawing/UI/Tech/Programming tags. Lists, tag filtering, detail pages and related links; no search, comments or subscriptions in the first version. Preserve existing biography and project facts/links; correct spelling only. System theme with saved manual override; remove artificial loading delays in the eventual redesign.

## Brand Commitments
Dark charcoal surfaces, diagonal texture, substantial rounded panel edges, pill controls and acid yellow-green emphasis, derived from the user's supplied game-interface reference. Home is a content-first personal space, Gallery an image-led exhibition, Writing an editorial reading space.

## Evidence on Hand
Six real projects and biography in existing TSX files. No drawings or writing supplied yet. Use honest empty states, never fictitious published content.

## Current Delivery
A / Street Poster selected from three image concepts on 2026-10-28 and implemented at the production root homepage. Oversized condensed typography, twin opposing printed ribbons, and Drawing/Writing panels extend the content-first direction; canvas and console remain excluded. Gallery/Writing empty states and Projects/About retain their existing content. The historical preview remains at /prototypes/index.html. See ADR 003 and DESIGN.md. Markdown rendering and content publishing remain future work.
